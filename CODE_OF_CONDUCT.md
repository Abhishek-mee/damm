# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want DAMM to be a place where anyone can contribute, regardless of experience, background, identity, or the model they happen to use. We pledge to make participation harassment-free and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about the work and generous with the person.
- Critique the change, not the contributor.
- Assume a good-faith attempt until the evidence says otherwise.
- Ask before rewriting someone else's lane.
- Accept that "I don't know" and "I was wrong" are normal.
- Respect the time of reviewers: small diffs, clear descriptions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews, or discussions.
- Dismissing a report of a problem without reading it.
- Using AI-generated volume to drown out a human review.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commit messages, and any space where you represent DAMM.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers at the address in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), or open a private report through GitHub if available. Reports are read by a human. We will not publish your identity without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject a contribution that breaks these rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban a participant for repeated or severe behavior. Decisions are explained to the person affected.
